- correspondent: the organisation or person the document is from or about (bank, utility, employer, clinic, government agency, landlord, shop). Write the name as the document writes it; do not translate or transliterate. If LEARNED HINTS name a known correspondent that matches, use exactly that name. Use "" if unknown.
- document_type: the form of the document, exactly one of: invoice (a bill asking for payment), receipt (proof that a payment or purchase happened), statement (a periodic account summary), contract (a signed agreement or binding terms), tax-return (a declaration filed with a tax authority), tax-assessment (a tax authority's computation, bill or notice), payslip (salary slip or employer income statement), certificate (certifies a fact, qualification or registry entry), attestation (a confirmation of someone's status or a fact), id-document (passport, ID card, licence, permit card, personal number card), letter (correspondence or notification), application (a form being submitted), policy (insurance policy or card), medical-report (clinical results, reports, discharge summaries), prescription, ticket (travel ticket or booking), license (software licence), manual, quote (estimate before a purchase), legal (court or notarial instrument). Use "other" only when none of these fits.
- document_date: the issue or creation date of the document as YYYY-MM-DD, or "" if there is none. Never use a due date, a birth date or the end of a billing period unless it is the only date. Prefer a date listed under DETECTED DATES.
- period_year: the fiscal or reporting year the document refers to when it differs from the document date (tax returns, tax notices, annual statements, income certificates), as YYYY; otherwise "".
- language: the main language of the document.
- title: 3–8 words in the document's own language that say what this specific document is, without date or correspondent. Examples: "Fatura eletricidade julho", "Выписка по счёту за январь", "Lease agreement flat 5B".
